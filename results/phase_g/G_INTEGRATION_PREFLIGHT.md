# Phase G integration preflight

Status: `G_INTEGRATION_BLOCKED` by codec/task incompatibility.

Before any smoke arm, durable exact-protocol evidence must cover eager/JIT
contracts, fail-closed routing/loss/update, sanitized-target leakage, carrier
identity and reactivation, a finite full-model gradient/update with frozen
teacher and rho, focused lint, public dependency boundaries, codec compatibility,
and the corrected score oracle.

The isolated worktree intentionally contains no shared ignored checkpoints.
Before runtime gates, the runner must copy the exact hash-verified F1 and codec
artifacts into this worktree with its `import-artifacts` stage. Symlinks and
shared mutable run/evidence paths are forbidden. The durable import record must
contain resolved source/copy paths and separately computed matching hashes;
nonidentical destinations are rejected without overwrite.

All runtime evidence must name the immutable committed G implementation SHA.
The runner rejects tracked source changes and untracked non-evidence files, so
gate results cannot silently straddle implementation revisions. Evidence-only
JSON/JSONL may accumulate under the same manifest until the final evidence
commit.

After the teacher arm, correct structure must separately beat shifted cuts,
permuted extents, joint shifted/permuted structure, and fixed neutral structure.
For every control it needs at least +1 percentage point byte accuracy and at
least 2% lower generated-length loss, with paired bootstrap intervals favorable
for both metrics. Controls are not averaged.

The source-bound corrected oracle passed 21/21 exact and 21/21 Monte Carlo
cases, but the prerequisite codec gate failed before full-model engineering
preflight. No teacher, argmax, or sampled arm is permitted to start.
