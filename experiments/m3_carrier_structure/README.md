# M3 carrier structure and structural corruption

M3 extends the validated M2 direct carrier with fixed-shape boundary and
expansion-length targets. The primary configuration is `C=6`, `d=3`, `L_max=3`,
and Q=1.

Each example is generated by a fixed-capacity segment scan. Site zero is always
a boundary. Later sites begin a new logical chunk with probability 0.35. A new
chunk receives an independent three-dimensional latent; non-boundary sites
carry a small latent drift. Content is the chunk latent plus a position effect,
a chunk-type effect, and keyed Gaussian noise. Chunk type is categorical in
`0..3` and is the clean expansion length, so length zero is a real target class
and occurs independently through the sampled chunk type. Boundary and length
share chunk information but are not encoded as copies of one another. The
content discontinuity at a new chunk is intentionally recoverable by the local
readout `[h_i, h_{i-1}, h_i-h_{i-1}]`, even when boundary observations are fully
UNKNOWN.

Content corruption is `alpha*h + sigma*epsilon`, with M2's medium values
`alpha=0.6`, `sigma=0.5`. Structural corruption independently replaces b or
length with an explicit UNKNOWN state with probability `rho_b` or `rho_length`.
UNKNOWN is not a valid target class and is distinct from length zero.

The Torx-native Q=1 `AffineGaussianGate` updates only h. Small shared per-site
affine heads read local predicted h and one-hot observed structural states to
predict b and length. Predictions never enter the Torx core, routing, topology,
or shapes. Structural targets use ordinary cross-entropy; the M1.5 score bridge
is not used.

The predeclared full-UNKNOWN gate is computed from the frozen validation class
distributions before final runs: boundary non-forced UNKNOWN accuracy must be at
least the non-forced majority baseline plus 0.10 and UNKNOWN F1 must be at least
0.30; length UNKNOWN accuracy must be at least its majority baseline plus 0.10.
Length-zero UNKNOWN accuracy is reported separately and is required to be above
0.25 when at least 20 UNKNOWN zero targets are present.
